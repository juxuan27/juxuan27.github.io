Biography

Hi, I'm Xuan Ju (鞠璇). I am a final-year Ph.D. student at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. I am also a Member of Technical Staff at Microsoft AI, where I work on image generation. Before this, I majored in Computer Science and Technology (minored in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics) and got my bachelor's degree from Tongji University.

I have a broad interest in most areas of computer vision, especially in image/video generation and multi-modal models. Please do not hesitate to reach out to me via email at juxuan.27@gmail.com!
